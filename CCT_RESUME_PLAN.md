## Audience and constraints

- Reader is an RWJBH nurse manager or recruiter screening for BLS, reliability, and comfort with people. Roughly a 20-second read.
- One page. Plain section names, no tables or multi-column tricks, so the applicant tracking system parses it.
- Unit-neutral: one file reused for every posting.
- No em dashes.

Applying broadly, not picky. Units heard about from current CCTs: Surgical Unit, Pediatrics, ICU, Cardiac ICU, Med Surg, Neonatal ICU, Emergency Department, Post Coronary, Orthopedics, Renal Transplant, RDU.
